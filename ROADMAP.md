## Build sequence (phase-gated)

- **M0 — Scaffold + ratify.** Doc moved in as `methodology.md`; harness added;
  sibling relationship to autonomous recorded. *Gate: human ratifies the
  manifest, the project's identity (methodology, downstream consulting
  offering), and this roadmap.* **← current**
- **M1 — Doc integrity pass.** `./verify` doc-lint green over the existing
  document (anchors resolve, section numbering clean, claims cited-or-marked).
  Verify the one flagged unverified primary (DELEGATE-52) or mark it clearly
  as attributed. *Gate: verify full green; no unflagged empirical claim.*
- **M2 — Demonstration strategy (Part VI).** Currently a placeholder. Develop
  and select the demos (XY-problem, latent-space navigation, remediation-cliff)
  — build each as a concrete, reproducible before/after. *Gate: at least one
  demo runnable/presentable end-to-end.*
- **M3 — Engagement materials.** The Operational Diagnostic as a deliverable
  template; case studies rendered to portfolio form. *Gate: a diagnostic could
  be run for a real client from the materials alone.*
- **M4 — Legibility / translation layer.** The doc's own Stage 5 applied to
  itself: render across registers (a short pitch, a one-page diagnostic
  summary, the full treatise) so the insight isn't trapped in one form.
  *Gate: three registers exist and agree.*
