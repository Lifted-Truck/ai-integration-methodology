# ai-integration-methodology

**The Applied Epistemics of AI Integration** — a methodology for rigorous
human-AI collaboration, and the seed of a consulting offering. The human-
epistemics half of the practice; sibling to
[autonomous](https://github.com/Lifted-Truck/autonomous) (the agentic/
deterministic half).

## The core idea, in one line
AI is optimized to give you what you're looking for, and you are optimized to
stop looking once you find it; the methodology is the discipline that keeps that
dynamic from quietly warping your model of reality — while still reaching the
genuinely novel knowledge conventional use can't.

## Relationship to autonomous
Siblings, not dependency. autonomous enforces friction *in the machine*
(oracles, hooks, critics); this project owns the friction *in the human* (the
governing principle, designed adversarial stress-testing, anchored compression).
autonomous has harvested this project's citable grounding (DELEGATE-52 + the
failure taxonomy) into its research and a doctrine tenet; the full treatment
lives here.
